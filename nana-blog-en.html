★ HeYyY, iT's Me !!!

Hello! I'm Léna de Firmas, aka Nana <3

I'm an illustrator, author and freelance music and digital communications specialist. I draw, make animations, write articles about musical instruments and music-making, and produce music-related videos, including face-cam videos. I also help artists and brands with their communications.

I have a soft spot for people who are a little out of step, slightly wonky stories and ideas that pop up out of nowhere. For example, at first I just needed a portfolio, and now there's an entire bedroom to explore. You can see it here.

I'm currently writing and illustrating La Princesse Clown, a fairy tale for adults about the difficulties of childhood, what we carry with us and what it means to grow up.

This is where my professional projects meet my personal obsessions. Apparently I'm a little unusual. That doesn't stop me from taking my work very seriously, sometimes a little too seriously.

Looking for an illustrator, writer or creative collaborator? Write to me!

★ My LiFe !!!

01. A few (way too many) schools

Literary baccalaureate (art history specialisation), two years of preparatory classes, a history degree, a year at the École du Louvre, admission to a master's programme at Sciences Po Paris, then training in web development and music/digital communications. I'll admit, it might look like I got lost. But really, I was exploring different paths to find myself. And it worked!

02. Then I decided to work in music

I've always loved music. Then, in 2019, a new obsession: getting behind the scenes to see how it all works.

After changing career paths, I ended up working in music journalism and communications with Mouv', La Pépite, Calvi on the Rocks, Pyrène and Live Me If You Can.

03. Currently juggling a few tabs

I'm currently freelancing with Expressive E, Gearnews.fr and jazz pianist Tomasz Bura. I'm also working on Nana Color Studio and my illustrated book La Princesse Clown.
