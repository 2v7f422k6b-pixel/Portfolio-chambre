★ HeYyY, iT's Me !!!

Hey! I'm Léna de Firmas, aka Nana.

I'm an illustrator, author and freelance music and digital communications specialist. I make illustrations, write articles about musical instruments and music-making, produce videos and help artists and brands communicate.

I have a soft spot for slightly odd characters, stories somewhere between humour and melancholy, and ideas that start small and end up taking over everything. I only needed a portfolio. Somehow, it turned into an entire bedroom.

I also write and illustrate La Princesse Clown, an illustrated fairy tale for adults.

This portfolio is where my professional projects meet my personal obsessions. I take my work seriously. My interests? A little less conventional.

Looking for an illustrator, writer or creative collaborator? Write to me!

★ My LiFe !!!

01. A few (way too many) schools

Literary baccalaureate (art history specialisation), two years of preparatory classes, a history degree, a year at the École du Louvre, admission to a master's programme at Sciences Po Paris, then training in web development and music/digital communications. It might look like I got lost, but I was really exploring different paths to find myself.

02. Then I decided to work in music

I've always loved music. Then, in 2019, a new obsession: getting behind the scenes to see how it all works.

I've worked in music journalism and communications with Mouv', La Pépite, Calvi on the Rocks, Pyrène and Live Me If You Can.

03. Currently juggling a few tabs

I'm currently freelancing with Expressive E, Gearnews.fr and jazz pianist Tomasz Bura. I'm also working on Nana Color Studio and my illustrated book La Princesse Clown, which you can explore here.

★ WhAt I dO ★

Illustration, animation & art direction: I create digital illustrations, characters and artwork for books and communication projects. I also make animations and develop visual identities.

Writing, journalism & web content: I write articles, especially about music, instruments and music software, plus scripts and illustrated stories. I also work on SEO and web writing, including keyword research, content optimisation and internal linking.

Communications, video & monitoring: I manage social media for artists and brands, plan editorial calendars, design campaigns and analyse results. I shoot and edit videos

Website creation: I design and build websites using WordPress, Cargo, Wix, HTML, CSS and JavaScript (qualified in front-end development). I also work on SEO.

Certification: Opquast, Web Project Quality (advanced level), awarded December 2018, expired December 2023. Covers web quality, accessibility, usability and digital best practices.

Analytics & reporting: I track performance metrics, prepare dashboards and performance reports to improve content and campaigns.
