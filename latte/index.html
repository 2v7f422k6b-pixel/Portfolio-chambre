♥Espresso→ ♥Vanilla→ ♥Steam→ ♥Pour→ ♥Art

Pull an espresso

Every latte begins with a little coffee magic. Extract a double espresso into your mug.

RECIPE NOTE ♥ 36–40 ml espresso + 15 ml vanilla syrup + as much steamed milk as you like.

Add vanilla syrup ♡

Sweeten your double espresso with 15 ml of vanilla syrup, then give it a gentle stir.

RECIPE NOTE ♥ Vanilla goes in before the milk so it blends right into your latte.

Steam the milk

Choose your milk, then tap the steam button three times to make lovely microfoam.

BARISTA TIP ♥ Keep the tip just below the surface for a smooth, glossy foam.

Pour your milk

Choose how much milk you like, then continue whenever you’re ready.

BARISTA TIP ♥ Pour slowly, then bring the pitcher closer to the surface.

Design your own latte art!

Draw whatever you imagine directly on the foam, with your finger, Apple Pencil or mouse.

Your latte is ready!
